## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-end wellness product aesthetic grounded in pharmaceutical rigor and consumer tech clarity. Inspired by Apple-grade industrial product reveals, the design balances clinical precision with an airy, elevated daily ritual.

### Emotional Intent
- **Purity & Efficacy:** Pristine surfaces, calm atmospheric gradients, and translucent glass layers convey clinical cleanliness without hospital sterility.
- **Duality of Circadian Rhythm:** A balanced transition between Day (bright, soft cerulean, energizing daylight) and Night (deep obsidian-navy, restorative twilight, quiet focus).
- **Tactile Modernism:** Pill-shaped capsules, soft container radii, metallic foil reflections, and micro-delight transitions that evoke modern consumer hardware.

### Design Movement: Modern Minimalist Glass & Tech-Wellness
The aesthetic marries **High-Contrast Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Glassmorphism**. Structural cards sit on soft radial ambient glows with micro-borders (`1px` translucent lines) and subtle dropshadows. High-density typographic hierarchies and pill-shaped structural hulls provide an ultra-clean, intentional feel.

## Colors

The color system captures the dual nature of circadian supplementation through a light-to-dark spatial progression.

### Primary Roles
- **Primary (`#061A44`):** Deep oceanic navy anchoring high-contrast typography, interactive buttons, and structural boundaries.
- **Secondary (`#2F5BD6`):** Vibrant electric cobalt used for focal CTAs, active selections, focus rings, and forward navigation cues.
- **Tertiary (`#8EAAFF`):** Luminous periwinkle providing highlights and accents on dark surfaces.
- **Neutral (`#E3EEF9`):** Soft, tinted sky base that replaces stark white to eliminate screen glare and introduce atmospheric depth.

### Day vs. Night Spectrum
- **Day Pack Surfaces:** Linear gradient `160deg, #A9CBEF 0%, #CFE2F6 100%` paired with deep `#0B2654` typography and subtle white inner specular highlights.
- **Night Pack & Survey Surfaces:** Linear gradient `160deg, #0B1226 0%, #16244A 100%` with `#F4F7FC` typography and `#22304F` structural containment borders.
- **Hero Cinematic Backdrop:** Near-black internal media canvas (`#050811` to `#0A1226`) strictly reserved for the silvery-blue sachet burst presentation.

## Typography

The typographical pairing unites functional Swiss modernism with technical precision:

- **Inter (Primary Text & Display):** High-density geometric clarity designed for crisp rendering across retina and mobile devices. Tight optical tracking on headings (`-0.045em`) provides an authoritative editorial posture akin to luxury technology announcements.
- **Manrope (Clinical Meta & Eyebrows):** Monospaced in spirit with open geometry, deployed with wide tracking (`0.18em`) and full uppercase transformation for eyebrows, ingredient dosages, metric tags, and step sequences.
- **Line Length & Rhythm:** Editorial lead-ins are capped at `56ch` to ensure effortless legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an Apple-inspired rhythm emphasizing generous negative space, deliberate grouping, and contained maximum widths.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Max Canvas Width:** `1080px` centered for primary section containers.
- **Narrow Editorial Width:** `720px` centered for section intros, headline groups, and the dark survey flow.
- **Vertical Spacing Cadence:** Standard desktop sections use `6rem` (`96px`) padding top and bottom. Mobile sections scale down to `3.5rem` (`56px`) vertical padding and `1.375rem` (`22px`) horizontal gutter margins.

### Breakpoints & Reflow
- **Breakpoints:** `380px` (compact mobile), `700px` (phablet), `768px` (tablet portrait), `900px` (tablet landscape / desktop collapse).
- **Principles Grid:** 4-column inline on desktop (`>900px`), collapsing cleanly to a stacked 1-column layout on mobile.
- **Founding Benefits Grid:** 4-column top row + 2-column bottom row on desktop; 2-column grid on tablet; 1-column stack on mobile.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent planar layering, soft specular edge lighting, and ambient colored drop shadows rather than heavy skeuomorphic cast shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Base sky-blue backdrop (`#E3EEF9`) illuminated by soft ambient radial glows (`radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 0%, rgba(255,255,255,0.8), transparent 70%)`).
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Light cards (`#F3F8FD`) bound by crisp `1px` translucent borders (`#C4D8EE` at `80%` opacity). Shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(6, 26, 68, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Elements & Floating Header):** Frosted glass layer using `rgba(227, 238, 249, 0.75)` backdrop blur (`16px`), `1px` border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`), and an ambient soft shadow `0 12px 36px rgba(6, 26, 68, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Dark Waitlist Stage & Modals):** High-contrast obsidian surface (`#111C36`) encased in `1px solid #22304F` with deep ambient diffusion `0 24px 64px rgba(5, 8, 17, 0.4)`.

### Hero Stage Direction (Sachet Burst)
The hero stage uses an inner cinematic dark viewport (`#0A1226`) with a subtle top edge specular highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`). Metallic light interaction on the sachet foil and suspended supplement softgels generate natural foreground-to-background separation without synthetic drop shadows.

## Shapes

The shape system is pill-dominated, echoing the form factor of pharmaceutical softgels and modern tech accessories.

- **Buttons & Chips (Fully Rounded Pill):** Radii set to `9999px` to create distinct, tactile, pill-shaped hulls.
- **Cards & Visual Stages (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (`24px`) to `2rem` (`32px`) corner radius, softening large surface areas while preserving structural discipline.
- **Accordions & Option Tiles (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (`16px`) radius, balancing interior density with ergonomic tap targets.
- **Form Controls:** Fully rounded pill inputs or `1rem` rounded selector blocks to maintain soft touch ergonomics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), gradient background `linear-gradient(180deg, #0B2654 0%, #06163B 100%)`, text `#F4F7FC`, trailing inline arrow icon. Minimum interactive size: `48px` height, `24px` horizontal padding. Hover: subtle `-1px` vertical lift with increased shadow bloom. Active: scale `0.98`. Focus: `2px` solid `#2F5BD6` with `2px` offset.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1px solid #C4D8EE`, text `#061A44`. Hover: background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`.
- **Dark Surface Button:** Pill-shaped, gradient `linear-gradient(180deg, #2F5BD6 0%, #1A3FB0 100%)`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Floating Header Navigation
- Compact pill-shaped bar anchored `16px` below top viewport.
- Frosted sky glass background (`rgba(227, 238, 249, 0.8)` with `blur(12px)`), `1px` border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).
- Left: Authentic vector wordmark. Right: Pill CTA button (`Vote` / `Join Waitlist`).

### Product Pack Cards (Day & Night)
- **Day Pack:** Large rounded card (`2rem` radius) with `linear-gradient(160deg, #A9CBEF 0%, #CFE2F6 100%)`, `#0B2654` headers, and crisp pill-spec containers.
- **Night Pack:** Complementary card with `linear-gradient(160deg, #0B1226 0%, #16244A 100%)`, `#F4F7FC` headers, and `#22304F` border outlines.
- **Supplement Accordions:** Nested cards displaying custom pill render icons, supplement name, clinical dose, and benefit tags. Features a smooth `250ms` height transition and a `180deg` rotating chevron indicator.

### Survey & Waitlist Card
- Centered on the dark obsidian section (`#0A1226`).
- Step-by-step progress indicator: Manrope uppercase step counter + segmented pill progress bar.
- Option Tiles: Large selectable cards (`border: 1px solid #22304F`, background `#111C36`). Selected state: `border-color: #8EAAFF`, background `rgba(47, 91, 214, 0.15)`, and a glowing accent radio checkmark.
- Inputs: Pill inputs with text `#F4F7FC`, placeholder `#6D7B9A`, and high-contrast validation states (success `#4ADE80`, error `#F87171`).

### Timeline Sequence
- Connected vertical progression with numbered circular nodes (`32px` diameter).
- Active node: Glowing cobalt dot with radiating ripple. Inactive node: `#C4D8EE` line with `#6F8FCB` numbers.
